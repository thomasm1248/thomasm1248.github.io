Instructions for you

Because I decided to keep my website simple and low-tech, it's not very interactive. That is, there are no like-buttons, comment sections, reposting mechanisms, or friend-requests. Rather than interacting with my content by clicking buttons and leaving comments, you must interact with me. Directly. In person or through email. This allows for a lot more flexibility than would otherwise be possible with social media platforms (feel free to think outside the box), and makes the internet more human.

Email me at thomasm1248@gmail.com

For friends and family, I also have a life-update mailing-list. This is my alternative to posting about my life on social media.
