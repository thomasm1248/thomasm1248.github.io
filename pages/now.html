Getting a flip phone

I went ahead and pulled the trigger on getting a flip phone. I chose the TCL flip 4 because it's more of a modern flip phone and because it has KaiOS.

Update: It arrived, and I've pretty much finished switching from my old Android phone to my new flip phone. How does it feel? Well, I've been feeling a strange dissatisfaction with my purchase. After tracking down where that feeling was coming from, I realized that it was because I simply don't like using the phone. And if you don't like using what you bought, then the money was poorly spent, right? WRONG!!! I realize now that this feeling of dissatisfaction is exactily what I paid for. Now I can be sure that I will be using my phone less.

Foster care

This has been an incredibly rewarding experience. A lot of people say that they couldn't do foster care, but you really just never know what you're capable of until you try. I've found to my surprise that once my wife and I got used to the extra responsibilities, it didn't feel so overwhelming anymore to suddenly be parents. And the benefit of doing something really beneficial (such as foster care) is that you feel like you're doing something really beneficial, which was something we really needed now that we're done with school.
