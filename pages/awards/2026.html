Index Cards

This year, I switched from carrying a pocket-notebook to carrying a deck of index cards. It's just like a pocket notebook, but you can...

  • Re-order the pages
  • Throw away pages you don't need anymore
  • Lay them out next to each other
  • Always have the perfect ratio: ~90% pages with useful information on them + ~3 ready-to-use blank pages

Bind them with a binder clip, wrap them in a leather wallet, and I'm all set!

Atomic Bomb pizza

I came up with this pizza flavor this year, and it's my new favorite flavor. It's a ranch, motzerella, chicken, jalapeno, mushroom (and sometimes tomato, onion, and/or bacon) pizza. Get it? Atomic bomb, because it's hot and has mushrooms? Pretty clever if you ask me...

Internet Journaling

Internet journaling is keeping a list of the best content, websites, articles, ideas, opinions, and etc that you encounter; and publishing it on the internet. Not only do you have a record you can go back to whenever you want, but it also makes you more self-conscious of what you're searching for on the internet, inspiring you to focus your attention on what matters.
