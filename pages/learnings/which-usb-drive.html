SanDisk isn't reliable anymore

TL;DR: SanDisk used to be a reputable thumb drive brand, but they got aquired, and it's been downhill ever since then. Buy a Samsung thumb drive instead.

I used to think that SanDisk was a pretty reliable USB drive brand. However, I recently bought one and it failed pretty quick. $20 down the drain. Anyways, I did some research and found that a few years ago, SanDisk got aquired by Western Digital, and ever since then, people on the internet are complaining about the abscence of customer service and the poor quality of the products. I guess I'm not the only one buying SanDisk to receive disappointment for my money. It sounds like there's quite a few people that swear by Samsung now. I guess I'll try them out. It's a shame that Walmart is still selling mainly SanDisk as if it's still a reputable brand.
