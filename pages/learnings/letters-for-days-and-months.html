Day & Month Abbreviations

I have often found myself wanting a way to use single letters of the alphabet to refer to days of the week and even months of the year. Here's what I use.

Days of the week

Universities typically use MWTRF, but they don't usually have a standard for Saturday and Sunday. Here's what I've come up with:

    M onday
    T uesday
    W ednesday
thu R sday
    F riday
    S aturday
  s U nday
