I want to make an business card

Oct 15, 2026

For whatever reason, I'm fascinated with business cards. I collect them from other people, and I'm increasingly wanting to have my own. Not just any business card though. Although the company I work for wants to help me make a business card I can give to our customers, I also want to just have my own business card that has nothing to do with my profession. I guess I see it as a sort of sophisticated analog social media. Here are my thoughs I've had so far:

  • I could have a bunch of logos on it that anyone with similar interests as me would recognize.
  • I could have a QR code on it that takes people to a page on my website that is specifically designed for people who are scanning my QR code.
  • Contact info. Duh.
  • Maybe a little message such as, "I really like business cards for some odd reason." No other explanation.
